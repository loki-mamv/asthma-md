Can a Steroid Inhaler Cause Oral Thrush, and How Do You Prevent It?

September 25, 2026 6 min read By thrive.md Editorial Team
Medical advisor: Agustin Arrieta, MD — Otolaryngologist (ENT), South Florida

The short answer: Yes. Inhaled corticosteroids can cause oral thrush, a yeast infection of the mouth and throat caused by Candida albicans. In the fluticasone propionate HFA label trials, 2% to 5% of people on the drug got mouth or throat candidiasis, compared with under 1% on placebo. The main prevention steps are to rinse your mouth with water and spit after every dose, use a spacer with an aerosol inhaler, and stay on the lowest dose that controls your asthma.

Why do steroid inhalers cause oral thrush?

An inhaled steroid is meant for the airways. But not all of each dose reaches the lungs. Some of it lands on the tongue, the inside of the cheeks and the back of the throat.

MedlinePlus explains that with poor technique, less medicine gets to your lungs and most remains in the back of your mouth. The steroid then lowers local immune defenses in that tissue. A 2025 narrative review in Healthcare describes thrush from inhaled steroids as the result of local immunosuppression in the oral cavity, which lets Candida overgrow.

MedlinePlus lists steroid medicine, including some inhalers for asthma and COPD, as a risk factor for thrush. Other listed risk factors include diabetes with high blood sugar, antibiotics, and dentures that do not fit well.

This applies to controller inhalers, not rescue inhalers. Albuterol is not a steroid. If you are not sure which of your inhalers contains a steroid, see our guide to rescue versus controller inhalers.

How common is thrush from a steroid inhaler?

The FDA labels give the clearest numbers from controlled trials. Each figure comes from a 12-week trial, so it describes short-term use.

  • Fluticasone propionate HFA (Flovent HFA and its generic): in two 12-week U.S. trials of 812 people aged 12 and older, candidiasis of the mouth and throat occurred in 4%, 2% and 5% at 88, 220 and 440 mcg twice daily. The placebo rate was under 1% (DailyMed label).
  • Budesonide (Pulmicort Flexhaler): in pooled 12-week trials in people aged 6 and older, oral candidiasis occurred in 1.3% at 360 mcg twice daily versus 0.4% on placebo (DailyMed label).
  • Fluticasone furoate (Arnuity Ellipta): in one trial, oral candidiasis and oropharyngeal candidiasis were each reported in 3% at 100 mcg, versus 0% on placebo (DailyMed label).

Real-world rates can run higher than trial rates. In a 2019 study of 186 adults with asthma using inhaled steroids, 19.4% reported having oral candidiasis at some point, and 5.4% had it at the time of the study. A Dutch prescription study of 52,279 new inhaled steroid users found antifungal prescriptions rose after the steroid was started. The risk was highest in the first 3 months, and higher daily doses predicted it (Drug Safety, 2013).

Can a steroid inhaler make your voice hoarse?

Yes. Hoarseness, called dysphonia, is the other common throat side effect. In the fluticasone propionate HFA trials, hoarseness or dysphonia occurred in 2%, 3% and 6% across the three doses, versus under 1% on placebo. The rate was highest at the top dose (DailyMed label).

Hoarseness is not always thrush. A 2012 review in Mayo Clinic Proceedings says reported rates of dysphonia with inhaled steroids range widely, from 5% to 58%. The authors suggest steroid deposited in the throat may affect the muscles or lining of the voice box (Mayo Clin Proc). A hoarse voice with no white patches can still be linked to your inhaler.

How do you prevent thrush from an inhaler?

Every inhaled steroid label reviewed here gives the same core instruction: rinse your mouth with water after each dose, and do not swallow it. The steps below reduce the risk. They do not remove it.

  1. Rinse and spit after every dose. The fluticasone propionate HFA label says to rinse with water without swallowing to help reduce the risk of thrush. MedlinePlus adds a gargle: rinse your mouth with water, gargle, and spit.
  2. Use a spacer with an aerosol inhaler. MedlinePlus says a spacer helps get more medicine into your airways. The Mayo review lists spacer use as a way to reduce dysphonia. You do not need a spacer for dry powder inhalers such as Pulmicort Flexhaler or Arnuity Ellipta.
  3. Use the right technique for your device. The Pulmicort Flexhaler label tells patients to inhale deeply and forcefully. Aerosol inhalers work best with a slow, deep breath. Our guide to inhaler types covers the difference.
  4. Stay on the lowest dose that works. The fluticasone and budesonide labels both say to titrate to the lowest effective dose once asthma is stable, to reduce side effects. Only your clinician should change the dose.
  5. Clean the spacer and care for dentures. The Mayo review suggests washing the spacer. The 2025 Healthcare review lists proper denture care among its prevention steps.

If you take a controller puff and then need a second puff, the rinse comes once, after the last one. Our post on how long to wait between inhaler puffs covers timing.

How is inhaler thrush treated, and when should you see a clinician?

MedlinePlus lists the signs of thrush as white, velvety sores in the mouth and on the tongue, some bleeding when you brush or scrape the sores, and pain when swallowing. The Pulmicort Flexhaler label asks patients to tell their provider about any redness or white patches in the mouth or throat.

A clinician or dentist can usually diagnose thrush by looking at your mouth. Per MedlinePlus, treatment options include:

  • an antifungal mouthwash (nystatin)
  • antifungal lozenges (clotrimazole)
  • antifungal pills or syrup, such as fluconazole or itraconazole

See a clinician if you notice white patches, if swallowing hurts, or if hoarseness lasts. Pain when swallowing can mean the infection has spread lower. For a general look at what mouth and throat symptoms can mean, symptom.md covers the basics. Get care right away if your asthma symptoms get worse at the same time.

Should you stop your inhaler if you get thrush?

No, not on your own. The fluticasone, budesonide and fluticasone furoate labels all say thrush should be treated with antifungal therapy while the inhaled steroid continues. They add that therapy may at times need to be interrupted, but under medical supervision.

The patient information for Pulmicort Flexhaler is direct: do not change or stop any medicines used to control your breathing problems unless your healthcare provider tells you to. The Flovent HFA patient information says not to stop, even if you feel better.

Stopping a controller can let asthma control slip. If thrush keeps coming back, ask your clinician about technique, a spacer, the dose, or a different device.

The bottom line

Steroid inhalers can cause oral thrush and a hoarse voice, because some of each dose stays in the mouth and throat. In FDA label trials, thrush affected a few percent of users, compared with under 1% on placebo. Rinse and spit after every dose, use a spacer with aerosol inhalers, and keep to the lowest dose that controls your asthma. If thrush develops, it is treatable with antifungal medicine. Keep taking your controller unless your clinician tells you otherwise.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does albuterol cause thrush?

Albuterol is a bronchodilator, not a steroid, so it is not the usual cause of inhaler thrush. Thrush is linked to inhaled corticosteroids, which are found in controller inhalers. Combination inhalers that pair a steroid with another drug also carry the risk.

Is mouthwash as good as rinsing with water?

The FDA labels for fluticasone and budesonide inhalers name plain water as the rinse. They say to rinse and not swallow the water. Ask your clinician before adding an antiseptic mouthwash to your routine.

What does thrush from an inhaler look like?

MedlinePlus describes white, velvety sores on the tongue and inside of the mouth. The sores may bleed a little when scraped or brushed. Some people also have pain when swallowing.

Can children using steroid inhalers get thrush?

Yes. The budesonide label trials included people aged 6 and older and reported oral candidiasis. The fluticasone label says a valved holding chamber and mask may be used for young patients, and older children can rinse and spit after each dose.

How long after starting an inhaler does thrush appear?

A Dutch study of 52,279 new inhaled steroid users found the risk was highest in the first 3 months after starting. It stayed raised for at least a year. That makes the first months a good time to build the rinse habit.
